# Kids / Youth Activities (clubs, classes, camps – חוגים)

> Facts as of 2026-11. Verify regulation before relying on it in code.

Entities: organization, activity/group (age range, coach, location, schedule, capacity), season/term, child, guardian(s) (multiple per child, divorced-parent visibility), enrollment (status: trial → active → frozen → left), session, attendance, fee plan (monthly/term, sibling discount, scholarship), payment, event/game, message.
Flows: registration form (guardian + child + health declaration + photo/media consent) → trial session → enrollment + payment → weekly attendance (one-tap list on phone, offline-tolerant) → cancellations/weather broadcast → end-of-term summary/certificate.
Must-haves: emergency contact + allergies visible to the coach on the field; waitlist; make-up sessions; holiday-aware calendar (Jewish holidays, school vacations); guardian-only access to their own children.
Israel: minors' data = sensitive (Privacy Law Amendment 13) – minimal medical info, coach-only access, consent stored with timestamp; health declaration (הצהרת בריאות) for sport; police certificate for staff working with minors (חוק למניעת העסקה של עברייני מין) – track expiry; insurance; marketing to parents needs consent.
Roles: owner/admin, coordinator, coach (own groups), guardian (own children), accountant.
KPIs: retention per term, attendance rate, trial→enroll conversion, collection rate, revenue per group.
